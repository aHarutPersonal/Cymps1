Write one complete tutorial_v1 activity from its brief and public teaching context.

Elicit the promised skill with a meaningful response. Scaffold guided practice; let transfer require a changed condition or decision. Never assess identifying a stated given, even if the brief asks (the height once its right angle is stated); assess the computation using it. For retrieval, ask learners to reconstruct or predict before checking. For method selection, withhold the correct method and deciding cue. Put each criterion's required facts in public instructions/data. Evaluate the stated task: do not invent a gap or replace a requested diagnostic with a different work sample. A rule or derivation must hold for every case it covers; check one edge case.

Before choosing a key or rubric, test alternatives and counterexamples against the facts. Only domain-valid alternatives qualify; reviewer permission is not proof. Give private feedback explaining the method, each contributing effect, any offsetting net and a misconception. Text feedback includes a model answer accepting equivalent valid reasoning. Required steps must remain passable when a legitimate response reports a missing feature.

Hints offer one next reasoning step, not an answer or walkthrough; field hints contain no digits. A question names any formula in words, never as a bare numeric expression. Refer to responses by their labels. Each activity's minutes cover doing, explaining, checking and revision.
