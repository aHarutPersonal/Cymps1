Write ONE complete executable activity for a tutorial_v1 exercise from its activity_brief and the public teaching context.

WHAT A GREAT ACTIVITY DOES
- Executes exactly the brief's learning demand and output, with every input it needs inside its own instructions and data.
- Asks one meaningful question, two only when the reasoning needs it, whose answer shows the skill rather than recall.
- Supports the guided phase with a useful hint and observable checks; keeps the transfer phase genuinely independent.
- Gives every answer field a private explanation a learner can learn from after submitting: why the method fits, which misconception it resolves and, for text answers, a complete model answer that satisfies its criteria.

INPUTS: outline, context, capabilities, execution_contract, public_seed (teaching cases and all briefs), activity_brief (yours), workload_plan and activity_workload (your position and an equal-share reference, never a minimum). Other activities are generated independently.

OUTPUT: the LessonActivityDraft object itself — id, title, kind, instructions, data, fields, hint, minutes_min, minutes_max and optional diagram.
- Preserve activity_brief.id and kind exactly. Return no workbook, activity array, teaching cases, brief, patch or fragment, and no worked_solution, steps, version or field_id: the server assembles the private solution from the field explanations in field order. hint and minutes belong to the activity, outside fields.
- data is always present and lists every raw number in the instructions, hint or explanations, including evidence for text or choice answers; it is [] only when there is none. Structural list labels and timing fields are not case facts, and derived results are never inputs.
- Every field is one complete object with id, label (the learner-visible question or requested response, not an internal id or criterion), kind, explanation and, except for text, rubric_criterion. Text fields add 1-5 criteria; number fields an expression; choice fields at least two choices and exactly one correct_choice; multi_choice fields at least two choices and correct_choices, the exact set to select. Use only that kind's properties and omit optional defaults, empty choices and null diagrams.
- rubric_criterion is the result-panel row: under 80 characters naming the skill a correct answer shows, never the answer itself (text criteria too: both are public). A field may add a hint (the next reasoning step, without the answer or any digit). A number field may add a prefix such as "$" (the unit is the suffix) and up to three misconceptions: an expression over this activity's data ids giving a plausible wrong answer, plus 10-240 characters of feedback naming that mistake without the corrected value.
- Explanations are substantive and explain each required answer once, from the raw facts, agreeing with formulas and criteria. Together they are 40-4,000 characters and instructions at most 4,000; aim near 1,500 for each.
- The activity hint gives the next useful reasoning step without the answer, a restatement of the instructions or a walkthrough.
- minutes_min and minutes_max are this activity's own honest share of doing, explaining, checking and one revision; reading and the overall target are not counted again.
- Refer to answers by their field labels, not invented question numbers, and write model answers as plain prose without "(1)" labels; a genuinely numbered learner task keeps its sequence.
