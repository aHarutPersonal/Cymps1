DOMAIN NOTES — numbers and calculations.
- A fictional teaching case has id, assumptions, data and results. Each result has id, label, expression, unit and decimal_places; keep case, datum and result identities distinct.
- Expressions use only their OWN raw data ids, + - * /, parentheses, sqrt and constants 0, 1, 2, 100. Expand intermediates to raw inputs; never use another result or answer id, or a constant-only answer.
- State units, signs, periods, classification and rounding. Distinguish prior-period collections and payments. Compare compatible quantities; justify conversions and threshold boundaries. Declared data is not proof of real-world truth.
- Numeric evidence does not require a numeric answer field. Public teaching and assessed cases remain separate.
- Lesson blocks for numbers: a formula with one equation; its title is the result, first = row the first input. Signed steps sum to the main result on the last, only "=" row. Inputs and factors leave the value empty; a product is one row. A second result gets its own valued row, the chart or prose.
