DOMAIN NOTES — numbers and calculations. Apply these whenever a case, datum or answer involves quantities.
- Teaching cases: default to ONE explicitly fictional case with stable raw facts, units and assumptions. Each case has exactly id, assumptions, data and results, and its own id even when it is the only case; a datum or result id is not a case id. Each result has id, label, expression, unit and decimal_places.
- Expressions use only the same case's or activity's OWN raw data ids with + - * /, parentheses and sqrt (plus the constants 0, 1, 2 and 100). Expand intermediate steps back to raw inputs instead of referring to another result or answer-field id. A number field needs a computable expression, not a constant answer.
- State classification, sign, unit, period and display-rounding assumptions. Distinguish current-period transactions from prior-period collections and payments. Check threshold inclusivity.
- Keep public teaching cases separate from independent-transfer inputs and answers, and keep public computed results out of transfer answers.
- A declared datum is not proof of real-world truth: keep supplied evidence or an explicitly fictional case. Numeric evidence alone does not require a numeric answer field or an arithmetic exercise.
- Lesson blocks for numbers: the reading shows the method as a formula, walks one teaching case as signed steps whose values add up to a final result, and usually adds a waterfall chart of those steps. Plan cases whose signed contributions reach that result.
