NUMERIC ANSWERS AND EXPLANATIONS — apply to every number field and numerical claim.
- Numeric answers are compared with the exact expression result at an absolute tolerance of 0.01 by default. Prefer exact answers and do not invent precision. If the task genuinely requires rounding, say so and set that field's tolerance explicitly; a teaching case's decimal_places does not set an activity tolerance.
- The server renders one complete substituted equation and its result for each number field, and the labels of fixed choice keys; it renders no separate intermediate calculations. Do not retype those equations or results. Explain what the method means, why each signed term belongs, the assumptions, and the misconception it resolves; for a reconciliation, name every contributing term, its sign and why it belongs.
- If a general symbolic formula helps explain a number field, and that field's expression contains exactly one literal number, a positive factor or divisor of 1, 2 or 100, and no function call, put the complete expression in its own paragraph with the same operators and grouping, using readable symbols for this activity's own raw quantities. Keep assignments, results, units and prose outside that paragraph. Multi-coefficient formulas keep their separate steps.
- Compute every numeric answer before returning. For an additional task-essential numerical claim that is not already rendered, write a separate sentence naming its quantity, signed value and exact declared unit (if any), then terminal punctuation, and explain its meaning; such claims still need independent evidence. Derive any unsigned magnitude explicitly and keep every negative sign.
- Preserve task-essential counts and quantities as digits, with their reasoning; numerical checking depends on them. Every raw quantity a private explanation uses is declared in the activity's data.
