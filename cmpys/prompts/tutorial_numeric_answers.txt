NUMERIC ANSWERS AND EXPLANATIONS
Numeric fields use the exact expression result with absolute tolerance of 0.01 by default. Prefer exact answers; when rounding is required, state it and set the field's tolerance. A teaching case's decimal_places does not set activity tolerance.

The server renders each number field's substituted equation/result and fixed-choice labels. Explain their meaning, assumptions, signed contributions and misconceptions without retyping those results. Every raw quantity shown, including each part of a total, is a datum.

A symbolic formula may stand alone when the field expression has exactly one literal positive factor/divisor (1, 2 or 100) and no function. Preserve operators/grouping with readable symbols for its own inputs; keep units, assignments, results and prose outside that paragraph. Other formulas keep separate steps.

An additional essential numeric claim needs its own sentence naming quantity, signed value and exact declared unit, followed by terminal punctuation and interpretation. It still requires independent evidence. Derive positive magnitudes explicitly; preserve negative signs and task-essential digits.
