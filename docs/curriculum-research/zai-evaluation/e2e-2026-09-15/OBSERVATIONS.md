- Final saved plan has 12 weeks, 24 tasks and exactly 4 stored hours per week. Its first course explicitly identifies and corrects the diagnostic cash mistake and requires supplied cases and in-app answers. This is a positive personalization result.

- The new-account tutor correctly explains $60 profit and -$40 cash, asks what changes on subsequent collection, and persists both messages on the server. Tone remains verbose and unnecessarily gendered/generalizing for a beginner.

## Lesson result and manual quality check

The lesson job ended failed with `Concurrent lesson generation failed: step_2: zai request failed (TimeoutError; finish=missing)`. It retained one readable lesson out of two. The first-ready time is 505.953 seconds, about 8 minutes 26 seconds after its pipeline began. The app shows an overall failure banner and also allows opening the completed first lesson.

The first lesson contains 2,682 words, seven reader parts and a 13-minute reading estimate. Its prose allocates about 90 minutes including practice. This is substantial text, but length is not evidence of correctness.

The Riverside worked example is arithmetically correct: cash +500, profit 1,080, difference 580. The independent Lakeside Coffee Cart example is wrong:

| Quantity | Published answer | Recalculated from supplied events |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash change | +555 | +290 |
| Profit | 610 | 570 |
| Profit minus cash change | 55 | 280 |

Correct cash change: `475 - 150 - 90 + 55 = 290`. Correct profit: `475 + 260 - 150 - 70 + 55 = 570`. Reconciliation: `260 + 90 - 70 = 280`. The published checking rubric gives the equipment adjustment the wrong sign and explicitly notes its own arithmetic does not reconcile, then instructs the learner to find a misclassification. This could penalize correct reasoning and falsely blame the learner.

Other internal inconsistencies: seven named definition terms are described as eight matches; six transfer-case transactions are described as seven classifications. Equipment depreciation is broadly deferred without a sufficiently explicit case assumption; the explanation should distinguish the immediate purchase from subsequent use/depreciation.

Verdict: **FAIL content quality**. The case's correct answer key must be derived from the supplied transactions and independently checked before publishing or generating a rubric. Merely meeting section/word-count/schema gates did not catch these errors. The complete original lesson is preserved in first-lesson.md, with the independent computation in arithmetic-audit.json.

Final practice result: two preparation attempts failed; no workbook, answers, attempts or hints were created. The UI remained on “This practice needs another preparation attempt.” No claim can be made that arithmetic validation caused the failures because the preparation endpoint does not persist the failure cause on the account record.

Final cold-start result: account and plan restore, but chat UI is empty although both test messages remain in the server export.
